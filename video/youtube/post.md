## Description

A closed loop is the unit of a compounding marketing system: data leaves one platform, gets
acted on in another, and **returns** as a converted customer or a sharper signal — so every
cycle makes the next one better. This video walks all twelve loops SMMT runs across Shopify,
Klaviyo, Meta/Instagram, and Amazon — attribution and lookalike re-seeding, abandoned-cart
recovery, server-side CAPI, suppression, predictive tiering, consent propagation, Amazon
Attribution, and the cross-platform reuse engine that ties them together.

Built for DTC operators and the agency engineers who wire these systems up. Two rules shape
everything: audiences only flow **outbound** to the ad platforms (you can't pull membership
back out), and Amazon returns no marketing email (retention happens on-platform). Watch to the
end for the one idea that makes the whole stack worth more than its parts. → Which loop should
we break down in depth next? Tell us in the comments.
